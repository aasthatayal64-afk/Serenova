ABOUT THE PROJECT

About SERENOVA

Prototype 1 — AI Literacy & Integrity Project

🌸 Project Introduction

SERENOVA is a digital mood and wellness journal designed primarily for college students. It provides a simple space for daily emotional check-ins, journaling and reflection.

🎯 Problem Statement

Students often experience changing academic and personal pressures but may not regularly pause to reflect on how they are feeling.

✨ Objectives

Encourage regular reflection, help students record emotional patterns and demonstrate responsible use of technology in a wellness-oriented application.

🎓 Target Audience

The primary target audience is college and university students interested in simple tools for reflection, journaling and everyday wellness.

💻 Prototype 1 Features

Mood tracking, journal entries, browser storage, simple mood statistics, visual summaries and predefined wellness suggestions.

🤖 AI & Integrity

Prototype 1 does not contain a genuine artificial intelligence model. Wellness responses use predefined JavaScript rules. AI-assisted development should be reviewed, understood and tested by the student team.

🔒 Privacy & Prototype Limitations

Mood and journal entries are stored using your browser's localStorage. They are not intentionally transmitted by SERENOVA to an external server.

However, localStorage is not encrypted secure storage. Users should therefore avoid storing highly sensitive personal, medical or confidential information in this prototype.
