ABOUT THE PROJECT

About SERENOVA

Prototype 2 — AI Literacy & Integrity Project

🌸 Project Introduction

SERENOVA helps students record emotions, write reflections and preserve meaningful moments.

🎯 Problem Statement

Students may not regularly take time to reflect on their emotions and daily routines.

✨ Objectives

Encourage self-reflection, emotional awareness and responsible technology use.

🎓 Target Audience

College and university students.

💻 Prototype 2 Features

Mood tracking, journaling, Polaroid memories, reflection streaks and gentle reminders.

🤖 AI & Integrity

This prototype uses predefined JavaScript rules, not an integrated AI model. AI-assisted code should be independently reviewed and tested.

🔒 Privacy & Limitations

Mood and journal data are stored in browser localStorage. Photos are stored locally using IndexedDB. These are not encrypted private vaults. Avoid entering highly sensitive information.

Reminders work while the website is open. They are not guaranteed after the browser closes.
