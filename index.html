ABOUT THE PROJECT

About SERENOVA

AI Literacy & Integrity Educational Project

🌸 Introduction

A student-focused digital mood and wellness journal.

🎯 Problem Statement

Students may not regularly reflect on their experiences.

✨ Objectives

Encourage reflection, self-expression and emotional awareness.

🎓 Target Audience

College and university students.

💻 Features

Mood tracking, journaling, memories, wellness activities and dashboards.

🤖 AI Integrity

AI-assisted development with human review and verification.

🔒 Privacy & Limitations

Entries are stored locally in your browser and do not synchronize between devices. Avoid entering highly sensitive personal information.
