ABOUT THE PROJECT

About SERENOVA

AI Literacy & Integrity Educational Project

🌸 Introduction

A student-focused mood and wellness journal.

🎯 Problem Statement

Students may not regularly reflect on their emotions.

✨ Objectives

Encourage reflection, emotional awareness and personal expression.

🎓 Target Audience

College and university students.

💻 Features

Mood tracking, journaling, stickers, memories, streaks and pastel themes.

🤖 AI Integrity

AI-assisted development with independent human review and verification.

🔒 Privacy & Limitations

Mood and journal entries are stored in browser storage. Photographs may be stored using IndexedDB. Information does not automatically synchronize across devices.

Avoid entering sensitive personal information. This website is an educational prototype.
